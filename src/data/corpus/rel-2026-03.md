# Release notes — March 2026 (platform v2026.03)

Completed rollout 2026-03-20. API changes ship under `Meridian-Version: 2026-03-12`.

## New

- **Instant Refunds GA.** Out of beta for UPI and cards on HDFC, ICICI, Axis, and SBI rails. Refunds flagged `speed: "instant"` credit in under 60 seconds at ₹4 + GST per refund; automatic fallback to normal speed (fee waived) when the beneficiary bank is offline.
- **Mandate pause/resume API.** `POST /v1/mandates/{id}/pause` and `/resume` for UPI AutoPay and e-mandates, with new webhooks `mandate.paused` and `mandate.resumed`. Customer-initiated pauses from UPI apps now surface in the Dashboard instead of appearing as silent charge failures.
- **Dispute evidence API.** Programmatic evidence upload via `POST /v1/disputes/{id}/evidence` (previously dashboard-only). Multipart, 10 MB cap.

## Improved

- Payment lifecycle: `payment.captured_late` events now include `original_failure_reason` so recon jobs can classify flip-flops without a second fetch.
- Checkout v3.3: saved-card CVV entry reduced to a single keystroke flow; measured +1.8pp conversion on returning users.
- Sandbox: new test VPA `pending@mrdnsandbox` holds a payment in `pending` for 10 minutes to exercise timeout paths.

## Feature flags

- `instant_refund_auto_fallback` — default on.
- `dispute_evidence_api` — default on for all accounts with disputes access.

## Deprecations

- `POST /v1/refunds/instant` (beta endpoint) removed 2026-06-30; use `POST /v1/refunds` with `speed: "instant"`.
- Webhook signature `v0` scheme (SHA-1): rejected after 2026-09-01. Migrate to `v1` HMAC-SHA256 now.
